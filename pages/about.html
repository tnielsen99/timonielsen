After 11+ years building cutting-edge ML systems at McLaren Automotive (yes, the supercar company), I had a realization: the same data methods that optimize $300,000 engines can transform small businesses—but nobody was making it accessible. Enterprise companies have data teams. Small businesses have you—the owner, wearing 12 hats, making decisions with gut feel and scattered spreadsheets. That's what I fix. I bring enterprise-grade data analytics to growing companies, without the enterprise price tag, timelines, or complexity. Your data is already telling you what to do next—you just need someone to translate it.

Expert in building prediction systems that actually work in real business environments— not just academic exercises. From messy spreadsheets to automated insights, tested and proven with your actual data.

From messy data to clear decisions. I build practical systems that predict what's next—so you can plan with confidence instead of reacting in panic.

(a)
Diagnose
What's actually in your data? Where are the opportunities? What's realistic given your budget and timeline? I spend 1-2 weeks understanding your business and data landscape before recommending anything. Sometimes the answer is "You don't need me yet"—and that's okay.
(b)
Build & Test
I build the system, test it against your real data, and refine until it's delivering accurate, actionable insights. You see progress every week, not after months of black-box development. Typical build time: 4-8 weeks.
(c)
Launch & Train
I hand over a working system with clear documentation, train your team (even if that's just you), and make sure you're confident using it. You own everything—no ongoing dependencies unless you want support.
