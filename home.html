Common Questions

What if we don't have much data?

Most small businesses have more useful data than they realize. Even basic sales records, customer interactions, or operational logs can reveal valuable insights. I'll help you work with what you have and show you what's worth tracking going forward.

How hands-on do we need to be?

You'll need to dedicate 2-4 hours weekly during the project: answering questions about your business, reviewing outputs, and learning the final system. I handle all the technical work—you provide the business context.

What happens after delivery?

You own everything: code, dashboards, documentation. I train your team to use and maintain it. No recurring fees required. Many clients return for additional projects once they see the ROI, but there's zero obligation.

Why not just hire a data analyst?

A full-time analyst costs $80K-$120K/year. My projects deliver specific, high-value systems for $8K-$35K that your team can run independently. You get enterprise expertise without the enterprise overhead.
