## THE CORE PROBLEM YOU'RE SOLVING

**Current State:**
Your portfolio speaks to people who already understand data science. Small businesses don't.

**The Issue:**
- Small businesses search for "help with sales forecasting," not "ML consultant"
- Your credentials (McLaren, 11+ years, Gaussian Processes) intimidate rather than reassure
- No relatable examples (all McLaren enterprise work, no small business proof)
- Missing small business trust signals (pricing, timelines, risk reversal)
- Technical language creates barrier ("DOEs," "model validation," "Scikit-learn")

**The Opportunity:**
You have something rare: **enterprise-level expertise willing to work with small businesses.**

Most data consultants either:
1. Target only enterprise (you're too small)
2. Target small business but lack credentials (you're taking a risk)

You can offer: **"McLaren-grade analytics at small business scale."**

## COMMON QUESTIONS

### "Should I remove my McLaren experience?"

**No!** It's your credibility. Just reframe it:

- **Don't say:** "11+ years building ML systems at McLaren"
- **Do say:** "Enterprise expertise, small business focus. 11+ years at McLaren—now bringing those same methods to growing companies at prices that make sense."

### "What if I don't have real small business clients yet?"

Create **hypothetical case studies** based on common scenarios:
- Label them: "Representative example" or "Common scenario"
- Be transparent: "While I've focused on enterprise, this is a typical small business challenge I'm equipped to solve"
- Use industry knowledge to make them realistic

Then actively seek 1-2 pilot clients to replace with real stories.

### "How do I price pilot projects?"

**Pilot Project Pricing Formula:**
- Time: 2-3 weeks of work
- Value: $2K-$5K depending on complexity
- Goal: Prove value before full engagement
- Guarantee: Money-back if not valuable

**Examples:**
- Customer churn analysis: $3K
- Revenue forecast (1 metric): $2.5K
- Inventory optimization (pilot SKUs): $4K

### "What if my data is actually too messy to work with?"

**Be honest but positive:**
- Most small businesses have messy data
- Part of your value is cleaning/organizing it
- Minimum viable: 1-2 years of sales/customer data
- If truly not enough, offer "data readiness assessment" first

**Script:**
"Most clients think their data is too messy. In 11 years, I've seen it all. As long as you've been tracking sales, customers, or operations for 12+ months, we can work with it. Part of what I do is turn chaos into clarity."

### "Should I niche down to one industry?"

**Start broad, then niche based on results:**

**Phase 1 (Months 1-6):** Stay broad
- Target: Retail, Manufacturing, Professional Services
- Learn what resonates
- See which industries respond best

**Phase 2 (Months 6-12):** Start specializing
- Double down on 1-2 industries with best fit
- Create industry-specific landing pages
- Develop industry expertise

**Why:** You don't know which industry will be your best fit yet. Let the market tell you.

### "How do I get my first small business client?"

**4 Paths:**

**1. Warm Network (Fastest)**
- Email everyone you know who owns/runs a small business
- Offer free assessment ($0)
- Turn 1-2 into pilot projects ($2K)
- Get testimonials

**2. LinkedIn Outreach**
- Identify small business owners in target industries
- Engage with their content
- Send personalized message (not generic pitch)
- Offer free value (article, assessment)

**3. Local Networking**
- Chamber of Commerce events
- Industry meetups
- Small business associations
- Offer to speak on "Data for Small Businesses"

**4. Content Marketing**
- Write LinkedIn posts (use templates)
- Publish articles on LinkedIn
- Answer questions in small business forums
- Build authority, attract inbound

**Best combo:** Warm network + LinkedIn (2-3 hours/week)

### "What if someone wants a quote and I'm not sure what to charge?"

**Pricing Framework:**

**Discovery ($0):**
- 30-minute call
- Understand problem, data situation
- Honest assessment of fit

**Scope Definition ($0 or $500):**
- If complex: charge $500 for detailed scoping
- Deliverable: SOW with timeline, deliverables, price
- Credits toward full project if they proceed

**Pilot ($2K-$5K):**
- 2-3 weeks
- Focused problem
- Proof of value

**Full Project ($8K-$35K):**
- **$8K-$15K:** Single-problem solutions (churn prediction, inventory forecast)
- **$15K-$25K:** Multi-problem solutions (dashboard + 2-3 models)
- **$25K-$35K:** Platform/infrastructure (data warehouse, automated reporting)

**When unsure:**
- Ask for details
- Research similar projects
- Quote a range: "Typically $12K-$18K depending on complexity"
- Start with pilot to de-risk
